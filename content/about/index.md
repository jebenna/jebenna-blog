# Interesting... You Want to Know More About Me :)

My full name is **Yohannes Molla**, and I was born and raised in **Ethiopia**.

I've been interested in technology for as long as I can remember. As a child, I was always curious about how things actually worked — from TVs and satellites to phones and the internet. Whenever something caught my attention, I wanted to understand what was happening behind it. That curiosity eventually led me to where I am today.

## Where It Started

When I first got access to a computer in high school, I naturally spent more time exploring things that interested me than following the school curriculum.

I still remember convincing my IT teacher that **USB killers** existed and could damage a computer if you plugged one in. I barely understood the idea at the time, but I was fascinated by the fact that a simple USB device could potentially damage a computer by delivering more voltage than it could handle.

By the time I was in **Grade 12**, my sister got me a decent PC, and the first thing that came to my mind was **bug bounty hunting**.

I spent about a year trying to make it work while still dealing with school. Unfortunately, I didn't have much luck.

Looking back, I realize that I was mostly just a young guy who was fascinated by **hacking** and wanted to prove to my family that I could build a career from it. I wasn't mature enough to understand how much consistent learning and effort it actually required.

I jumped from one website to another, tried whatever scripts I could find, and followed tutorials without really understanding the deeper concepts behind what I was doing.

Eventually, my focus on bug bounty hunting affected my studies, and I failed my **Grade 12 exam**.

## College and a New Foundation

After that, I went to a local college and chose **Hardware and Networking Service (HNS)**.

In a way, that turned out to be a good thing.

College gave me the opportunity to slow down, learn new things, and build a proper foundation. I learned the basics of **networking**, gained some hands-on experience, and, more importantly, learned a few lessons about how I approach learning.

After graduating, I returned to the things I had left behind in Grade 12 — but this time with a better understanding of what I was actually trying to learn.

## Where I Am Now

I'm still learning, but I'm in a very different place compared to when I first started.

With the help of **AI** and the resources available today, I can take a concept that I don't understand, break it down, ask questions, experiment with it, and gradually go deeper until it makes sense.

I'm especially interested in **web security**, **Android application security**, **networking**, **software**, and understanding how systems work under the hood.

For now, I'm working on testing systems belonging to Ethiopian companies where I have permission to do so, building my security portfolio, and continuing to participate in bug bounty programs on platforms such as **HackerOne** and **Bugcrowd**.

I don't expect to know everything. I'm mainly interested in **learning, experimenting, and documenting** what I discover along the way.

That's what this blog is for.
